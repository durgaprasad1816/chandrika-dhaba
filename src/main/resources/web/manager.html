Google account & Drive

Sign in with the Gmail account that owns your Drive. Every paid invoice (PDF + payment photo) is saved to your Drive folder, and dish photos, waiter photos and Aadhaar pictures are stored in your Drive too.

Google app keys (one-time setup)
  1. Open console.cloud.google.com → create a project → APIs & Services → enable Google Drive API.
  2. OAuth consent screen: choose External, add your Gmail as a Test user.
  3. Credentials → Create credentials → OAuth client ID → type Web application.

  5. Copy the Client ID and Client secret here.

Open folder in Drive

Change this link any day you want invoices to go to a different folder. If you leave it empty, the app makes a folder called Chandrika Dhaba - Invoices in your Drive.

Automatic data backup

Dishes, waiters, settings and bills are copied to Drive (folder Chandrika Dhaba - Data / Backup) a few seconds after every change, and brought back automatically when the app starts on an empty disk (for example on Render's free plan).
